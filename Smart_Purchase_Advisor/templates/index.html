Abstract

The Core Idea

Our project aims to revolutionize the online shopping experience by providing an unbiased, transparent recommendation system. Conventional platforms often prioritize profit margins and affiliate commissions. The Smart Online Purchase Advisor uses AI-driven logic to prioritize the user's budget and preferences above all else.
